The mandatory pre-upgrade production database backup for the Elgg 4 → 5
upgrade point (Story S1.11 Step 8), executed per
`docs/deployment/deploy_runbook.md` Steps 1–2 on the VPS production host.

production upgrade is confirmed successful.

## Scope

The backup targets the VPS production database (the Coolify `dbm` container,
currently Elgg 4.3.10 carrying the 2026-11-04 old-live data snapshot) — not a
fresh dump of the old live site; the fresh live-site sync is deferred to
S1.13 per the story's Developer Notes and the runbook's production-topology
section.

## Notes

- The 32-table count is one above the S1.10 post-upgrade figure (31):
  consistent with the known growth mechanism — a system_log archive rotation
  since the 2026-11-16 deploy (the cron log shows
  `Elgg\SystemLog\Cron::rotateLogs` running on the VPS). The runbook rule
  (derive the expected count from the dump, never assume a fixed number)
  covered the delta without remediation.
- The `mariadb-dump`/`mariadb` "Using a password … insecure" warnings on
  stderr are expected and harmless (runbook).
- Retention: this dump is the Step 9 rollback point — retain until the
  production upgrade (Story S1.11 Step 9) is confirmed successful.
